# Electrocardiography (ECG)

Study of ECG signal generation, acquisition, conditioning, and visualization.

## Topics
- Cardiac depolarization and repolarization
- P wave, QRS complex, and T wave
- Limb leads and chest leads
- Instrumentation amplifier
- Right-leg drive circuit
- High-pass, low-pass, and notch filtering
- Opto-isolation
- Analog-to-digital conversion
